Privacy Policy - Unicare Healthcare Kenya

Last Updated: May 26, 2026

1. Introduction

Unicare Healthcare Kenya ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application and website.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Personal Information

  • Name and Username: When you create an account
  • Email Address: For account creation and communication
  • Phone Number: For M-Pesa payments and emergency contacts
  • Location Data: GPS coordinates to find nearest hospitals
  • Payment Information: M-Pesa transaction details

2.2 Device Information

  • Device type and model
  • Operating system version
  • Unique device identifiers
  • Mobile network information

2.3 Usage Data

  • App usage statistics
  • Hospital search queries
  • Features accessed
  • Time spent in the app

3. How We Use Your Information

3.1 Primary Purposes

  • Location Services: To find nearest hospitals with available beds
  • Payment Processing: To process M-Pesa payments for app access
  • Emergency Services: To provide location-based emergency contacts
  • Account Management: To manage your user account and access
  • Service Improvement: To improve app functionality and user experience

3.2 Legal Compliance

  • Comply with legal obligations
  • Protect our rights and property
  • Prevent fraud and abuse
  • Ensure security of our services

4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

4.1 Third-Party Services

  • M-Pesa: Payment processing (Safaricom)
  • Google Analytics: Usage analytics and app performance
  • Cloud Services: Data storage and hosting

4.2 Healthcare Partners

  • Hospital information (public data only)
  • Bed availability statistics (aggregated, anonymized)
  • Emergency contact information (public data)

4.3 Legal Requirements

We may disclose your information if required by law or to protect our rights, property, or safety.

5. Data Security

5.1 Security Measures

  • Encryption of data in transit and at rest
  • Secure authentication and access controls
  • Regular security audits and updates
  • Secure payment processing through M-Pesa

5.2 Data Retention

  • Account Data: Retained while account is active
  • Payment Records: Retained for 7 years (legal requirement)
  • Usage Data: Retained for 2 years for analytics
  • Location Data: Not stored permanently (session-based)

6. Your Rights and Choices

6.1 Data Access

  • Request access to your personal data
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your account and data
  • Opt-out of data collection (where legally permitted)

6.2 Location Services

  • You can disable location services in device settings
  • Disabling location will limit app functionality
  • Location data is only used with your consent

6.3 Account Deletion

  • You can delete your account at any time
  • Contact us at unicare@example.com to request deletion
  • Deletion will remove your personal data from our systems
  • Some data may be retained for legal or security purposes

7. Children's Privacy

Our application is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware of such collection, we will take immediate steps to delete it.

8. International Data Transfers

Your information may be transferred to and processed in countries other than Kenya. We ensure that such transfers comply with applicable data protection laws and that your data remains protected.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date.
